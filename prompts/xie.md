## Day 1, after writing A
```
Please review my Chinese text. Correct only major issues in these categories:
- grammar errors;
- wrong word choice that affects meaning;
- clearly non-native sentence patterns;
- bad collocations, including phrases that are understandable but clearly unnatural in Chinese.
Treat all four categories as major even if the meaning is still understandable. Do not downgrade a clearly non-native collocation or sentence pattern to “minor” just because it is not confusing.
“Minor” means only stylistic improvements: optional particles, punctuation preferences, synonym upgrades, rhythm, formality, and other acceptable-but-better alternatives. Skip those entirely.
For each major issue, give:
my phrase → corrected phrase → short why → 2 other natural examples that use the corrected pattern in different contexts, not examples from my text/context.
Do not label each item as “major” every time; I already understand that every correction you list is major.
Then estimate my HSK level with a short caveat.
Then give a natural rewrite ONLY if you made major corrections; otherwise say “No rewrite needed.”
If there are no major mistakes, say “No major mistakes” and do not invent corrections.

```
